About AstroPulse (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)

Built specifically for the challenge "Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions". AstroPulse empowers non-medical astronauts with real-time biometric telemetry, Solfeggio first-aid healing audio frequencies, and 3D ISS EVA separation protocols.

1. ONSD & SANS Ultrasound

Measures optic nerve sheath diameter (<5.0 mm normal) to detect microgravity intracranial fluid pressure shifts early.

2. i-STAT Microfluidic Blood Analysis

Painless finger-prick sample testing for electrolytes, blood glucose, and immune biomarkers in deep space transit.

3. NASA Human Research Program (HRP)

Covers the 5 hazards of human spaceflight: Radiation, Isolation, Distance, Gravity fields, and Closed environments.

ISS EVA Tether Separation & SAFER Protocol

When an astronaut detaches from ISS, heart rate spikes and EMU oxygen consumption triples. AstroPulse activates SAFER thruster simulation, stabilization algorithms, and 174 Hz calming audio frequency.
